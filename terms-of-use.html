3. Accuracy of nutrition values

The nutrition values in the app come from three different kinds of source, and they are not equally reliable:

  • Imported from international composition databases — their source and record number are traceable.
  • From product labels and community contributions — based on the manufacturer's declaration or on user input; not verified.
  • Reference values — a reasonable estimate, not copied from a composition table.

The AI's estimates from a photo are estimates too: portion size, cooking method and ingredients you cannot see change the result considerably. These values must not be relied on for any medical or clinical decision. No undertaking is given that the numbers shown match what you actually ate.
